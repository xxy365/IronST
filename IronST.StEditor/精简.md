# IronST.StEditor — 项目速查

WPF + AvalonEdit 的 IEC 61131-3 结构化文本(ST)编辑器控件(UserControl)。
不依赖 IronST.Frontend，语义数据通过 EditorAnalysisData 从外部注入。

## 文件结构
Editor/
  StCompletionData.cs        — 补全项(ICompletionData), CompletionKind枚举{Keyword,Type,Variable}
  StCompletionProvider.cs    — 补全源, 静态 GetCompletions(string,int,EditorAnalysisData?), AllKeywords集合
  StFoldingStrategy.cs       — 折叠策略, 13对 BlockPairs(PROGRAM~END_PROGRAM等)
  StSyntaxHighlightingTransformer.cs — 正则+块注释状态机高亮, RebuildLineStates/UpdateSyntaxColors/ColorizeLine
Features/
  IEditorFeature.cs          — 接口 OnTextEntered(TextEditor,string) + EditorFeatureBase抽象基类
  AutoAssign.cs              — ":" 自动补":=", IsInCodeContext(纯字符扫描,无ANTLR)
  AutoEndBlock.cs            — 块关键字snippet(IF/FOR/WHILE/REPEAT/CASE/PROGRAM/FUNCTION/FUNCTION_BLOCK/ACTION/TYPE)
  AutoUppercase.cs           — 关键字自动大写, 用AllKeywords集合查表+IsInCodeContext
  Completion.cs              — 补全窗口管理, AnalysisData属性, AfterInsert回调→AutoEndBlock
  StateRebuilder.cs          — 防抖200ms重建折叠+块注释状态
EditorAnalysisData.cs        — 数据契约: EditorAnalysisData{Variables,Functions,TypeDefinitions,Diagnostics}
                               SymbolInfo{Name,TypeName,Description,Line,Column}
                               DiagnosticInfo{Offset,Length,Message,Severity}
                               DiagnosticSeverity{Warning,Error}
StEditorControl.xaml/.cs     — 主控件, 功能模块链: _completion→_autoAssign→_autoUppercase→_autoEndBlock
                               依赖属性: Text(TwoWay), BaseFontSize, ZoomLevel, ShowLineNumbers,
                               IsReadOnly, EditorTheme(Dark/Light), NavigateToRequest,
                               UnderlineRanges, AnalysisData
                               事件: TextContentChanged
                               公共方法: GetEditor(), GoToLine(int), Select(int,int), Shutdown()
                               内部类: NavigationRequest(Line,Column), TextUnderlineRange(Offset,Length,Color)
                               SyntaxHighlightColors, ThemeColors, EditorThemes(Dark/Light Catppuccin)
                               SquigglyUnderlineRenderer(IBackgroundRenderer, 贝塞尔波浪线)

## 主题
Dark=Catppuccin Mocha(#1e1e2e底), Light=Catppuccin Latte(#eff1f5底)
补全窗口用 CompletionThemeColors.Dark/Light(独立配色)

## 事件流
按键→TextEntering(关补全窗口)→TextEntered→DispatchTextEntered遍历_enteredChain
→TextChanged→StateRebuilder.OnTextChanged(200ms防抖)→TextContentChanged(通知外部)

## 外部集成
外部监听TextContentChanged→防抖→调用自己的Pipeline→构造EditorAnalysisData→设MyEditor.AnalysisData
变量/函数/类型自动出现在补全列表, Diagnostics自动渲染波浪线
依赖: AvalonEdit 6.0+, .NET 6.0+ WPF, 无ANTLR依赖
